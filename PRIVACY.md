# Privacy policy

This is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of this repository.

- It reads the owner's own Gmail with the `gmail.readonly` scope. It never
  changes, sends or deletes mail.
- It reads bill mails in the inbox and compares their amounts with the
  owner's account book on the owner's own server.
- The results go only to the owner's own Telegram chat.
- No data is sold, shared, or sent to any other party.
- The OAuth token stays on the owner's server. To remove access, revoke it at
  https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/ypcat/zaim_telegram_bot/issues.
